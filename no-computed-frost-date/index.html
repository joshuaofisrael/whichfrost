Cities with no computed 32°F frost date

By Which Frost. Published 18 Nov 2026, from NOAA data retrieved 16 Nov 2026.

33 of the 341 cities on this site have no NOAA computed median last spring freeze at 32°F. All of them are in Arizona, California, Florida, and all show a 365 day median growing season at 32°F, meaning the station's own record rarely or never crosses that threshold.

These are 1991 to 2020 climate normals, not a forecast. A given year can still freeze. This is not agricultural advice.

For these stations NOAA did compute a milder 36°F last spring date, shown below, plus the growing season length used on the longest and shortest growing season page. The 32°F table on each city page still shows the 90 and 10 percent figures where NOAA published them.

Frequently asked questions

Why does my city's page say Not computed for the 32°F frost date?

NOAA flags an element -9999 when the station's history did not show that threshold crossed in enough of the 1991 to 2020 years to compute a median, or set a measurement flag of Y for the same reason. For these cities that is the last spring freeze at 32°F. The NOAA Normals Calculation Methodology 2020 document, linked in the sources below, defines those flags.

Does Not computed mean this city never freezes?

No single normals record can promise that. It means NOAA's own station file did not have enough qualifying years at 32°F to publish a median for that one element. NOAA did compute the milder 36°F threshold for the same stations, shown on this page, and the growing season length element separately.

Which states have cities with no computed 32°F date?

On this site: Arizona, California, Florida. That follows from which stations NOAA picked for frost products in this build, not a claim that every warm weather city in the United States is missing this element.
