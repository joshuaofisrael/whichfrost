USDA zone 4a: what to plant now

By Which Frost. Last verified 19 Nov 2026.

Zone 4a on the 2023 USDA Plant Hardiness Zone Map is an average annual extreme minimum of -30 to -25°F. It is not a frost date and not a sowing date.

Frost dates inside zone 4a

USDA zone 4a is a winter minimum band. The frost dates below are NOAA 1991 to 2020 medians at 32°F for the 1 large city on this site that fall in that band. They are medians, not guarantees for any single year.

The only city on this site in zone 4a is Fargo, North Dakota, with a median last spring 32°F freeze of May 4 and a median first fall 32°F freeze of October 2. NOAA's median growing season at 32°F is 151 days.

Frequently asked questions

Does USDA zone 4a tell me what to plant this week?

No. Zone 4a is the 2023 USDA average annual extreme minimum temperature, -30 to -25°F. Nurseries use it for which perennial plants can take a typical winter. Vegetable sowing dates on this site come from a state extension page counted against the city's NOAA frost date, when we have transcribed one.

What is the frost date for cities in zone 4a?

There is no single frost date for zone 4a. Each city page uses the nearest NOAA station with a frost product. The table on this page lists the median last spring and first fall 32°F dates for the cities in this zone on this site. Those dates are 1991 to 2020 medians, not a forecast.

Zone source: USDA Plant Hardiness Zone Map, ZIP tables from the PRISM Climate Group. A city's zone is the ZIP code whose centroid is nearest the city center. Frost dates: NOAA NCEI 1991 to 2020 climate normals.
