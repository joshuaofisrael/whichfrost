USDA zone 10b: what to plant now

By Which Frost. Last verified 16 Nov 2026.

Zone 10b on the 2023 USDA Plant Hardiness Zone Map is an average annual extreme minimum of 35 to 40°F. It is not a frost date and not a sowing date.

Frequently asked questions

Does USDA zone 10b tell me what to plant this week?

No. Zone 10b is the 2023 USDA average annual extreme minimum temperature, 35 to 40°F. Nurseries use it for which perennial plants can take a typical winter. Vegetable sowing dates on this site come from a state extension page counted against the city's NOAA frost date, when we have transcribed one.

Zone source: USDA Plant Hardiness Zone Map, ZIP tables from the PRISM Climate Group. A city's zone is the ZIP code whose centroid is nearest the city center.

Which Frost: Which Frost lists median last and first frost dates for U.S. cities from NOAA climate normals, with the USDA hardiness zone and a planting calendar only where a state extension page was transcribed. These are long term averages, not a forecast.
