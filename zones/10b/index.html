USDA zone 10b: what to plant now

By Which Frost. Last verified 19 Nov 2026.

Zone 10b on the 2023 USDA Plant Hardiness Zone Map is an average annual extreme minimum of 35 to 40°F. It is not a frost date and not a sowing date.

Frost dates inside zone 10b

USDA zone 10b is a winter minimum band. The frost dates below are NOAA 1991 to 2020 medians at 32°F for the 24 large cities on this site that fall in that band. They are medians, not guarantees for any single year.

Across 3 cities with a computed spring date, the earliest median last spring 32°F freeze is January 4 in El Monte, California. The latest is January 17 in St. Petersburg, Florida.

NOAA's median growing season at 32°F for cities in this zone on this site is 365 days (Los Angeles).

Frequently asked questions

Does USDA zone 10b tell me what to plant this week?

No. Zone 10b is the 2023 USDA average annual extreme minimum temperature, 35 to 40°F. Nurseries use it for which perennial plants can take a typical winter. Vegetable sowing dates on this site come from a state extension page counted against the city's NOAA frost date, when we have transcribed one.

What is the frost date for cities in zone 10b?

There is no single frost date for zone 10b. Each city page uses the nearest NOAA station with a frost product. The table on this page lists the median last spring and first fall 32°F dates for the cities in this zone on this site. Those dates are 1991 to 2020 medians, not a forecast.

Zone source: USDA Plant Hardiness Zone Map, ZIP tables from the PRISM Climate Group. A city's zone is the ZIP code whose centroid is nearest the city center. Frost dates: NOAA NCEI 1991 to 2020 climate normals.
