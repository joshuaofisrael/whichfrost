USDA zone 7a: what to plant now

By Which Frost. Last verified 16 Nov 2026.

Zone 7a on the 2023 USDA Plant Hardiness Zone Map is an average annual extreme minimum of 0 to 5°F. It is not a frost date and not a sowing date.

Frequently asked questions

Does USDA zone 7a tell me what to plant this week?

No. Zone 7a is the 2023 USDA average annual extreme minimum temperature, 0 to 5°F. Nurseries use it for which perennial plants can take a typical winter. Vegetable sowing dates on this site come from a state extension page counted against the city's NOAA frost date, when we have transcribed one.

Zone source: USDA Plant Hardiness Zone Map, ZIP tables from the PRISM Climate Group. A city's zone is the ZIP code whose centroid is nearest the city center.

Which Frost: Which Frost lists median last and first frost dates for U.S. cities from NOAA climate normals, with the USDA hardiness zone and a planting calendar only where a state extension page was transcribed. These are long term averages, not a forecast.
