USDA zone 8b: what to plant now

By Which Frost. Last verified 16 Nov 2026.

Zone 8b on the 2023 USDA Plant Hardiness Zone Map is an average annual extreme minimum of 15 to 20°F. It is not a frost date and not a sowing date.

What to plant now

Planting notes below are grouped by state, because extension calendars are not the same across a zone. With JavaScript on, the current month is marked inside any month list.

Georgia

UGA Extension Circular 943, written for middle Georgia. Cities north or south of that belt should read the caveat on the city page. The months are not shifted on this zone page. Source.

January

Plan the garden, order seed, and take a soil sample. Get plant beds ready so tomatoes, peppers, and eggplant can be seeded in early February.

February

Start seed boxes. UGA says peppers and eggplant take 8 weeks from seed to transplant size, and tomatoes take 6 weeks. Early plantings: carrots, collards, lettuce, mustard, English peas, Irish potatoes, radishes, spinach, and turnips.

March

Second plantings of turnips, mustard, radishes, and spring onions. Have rows ready for warm season crops in the last week of March or the first week or two of April, as the weather allows.

April

Plant frost tender crops: snap, pole, and lima beans, cantaloupe, sweet corn, cucumbers, eggplant, okra, field peas, peppers, squash, tomatoes, and watermelon.

May

A third planting of snap beans, corn, squash, and lima beans. Keep mulch and water in place for the crops already growing.

June

Plant sweet potatoes and a second planting of Southern peas. Harvest beans, peas, squash, cucumbers, and okra often.

July

UGA says to plant tomatoes, okra, corn, pole beans, lima beans, cucumbers, squash, and snap beans no later than July 20 if they are to mature before frost.

August

Snap beans and Irish potatoes no later than August 15. Cucumbers and squash no later than August 31. Start broccoli, cabbage, cauliflower, collards, kale, and onions for setting out in September.

September

Plant or transplant beets, broccoli, cabbage, carrots, collards, lettuce, mustard, onions, radishes, spinach, and turnips. UGA groups September and October together for this list.

October

Same cool season list as September: beets, broccoli, cabbage, carrots, collards, lettuce, mustard, onions, radishes, spinach, and turnips.

November

UGA's November and December notes are about manure, leaves, soil samples, and seed orders. They do not name a planting list.

December

Order next year's seed. UGA does not name a planting list for December.

Frequently asked questions

Does USDA zone 8b tell me what to plant this week?

No. Zone 8b is the 2023 USDA average annual extreme minimum temperature, 15 to 20°F. Nurseries use it for which perennial plants can take a typical winter. Vegetable sowing dates on this site come from a state extension page counted against the city's NOAA frost date, when we have transcribed one.

Zone source: USDA Plant Hardiness Zone Map, ZIP tables from the PRISM Climate Group. A city's zone is the ZIP code whose centroid is nearest the city center.

Which Frost: Which Frost lists median last and first frost dates for U.S. cities from NOAA climate normals, with the USDA hardiness zone and a planting calendar only where a state extension page was transcribed. These are long term averages, not a forecast.
