USDA zone 11a: what to plant now

By Which Frost. Last verified 16 Nov 2026.

Zone 11a on the 2023 USDA Plant Hardiness Zone Map is an average annual extreme minimum of 40 to 45°F. It is not a frost date and not a sowing date.

Frequently asked questions

Does USDA zone 11a tell me what to plant this week?

No. Zone 11a is the 2023 USDA average annual extreme minimum temperature, 40 to 45°F. Nurseries use it for which perennial plants can take a typical winter. Vegetable sowing dates on this site come from a state extension page counted against the city's NOAA frost date, when we have transcribed one.

Zone source: USDA Plant Hardiness Zone Map, ZIP tables from the PRISM Climate Group. A city's zone is the ZIP code whose centroid is nearest the city center.
