USDA zone 7b: what to plant now

By Which Frost. Last verified 19 Nov 2026.

Zone 7b on the 2023 USDA Plant Hardiness Zone Map is an average annual extreme minimum of 5 to 10°F. It is not a frost date and not a sowing date.

Frost dates inside zone 7b

USDA zone 7b is a winter minimum band. The frost dates below are NOAA 1991 to 2020 medians at 32°F for the 22 large cities on this site that fall in that band. They are medians, not guarantees for any single year.

Across 22 cities with a computed spring date, the earliest median last spring 32°F freeze is March 24 in Lubbock, Texas. The latest is April 28 in Reno, Nevada.

NOAA's median growing season at 32°F in this zone runs from 179 days in Albuquerque, New Mexico to 233 days in New York, New York.

Frequently asked questions

Does USDA zone 7b tell me what to plant this week?

No. Zone 7b is the 2023 USDA average annual extreme minimum temperature, 5 to 10°F. Nurseries use it for which perennial plants can take a typical winter. Vegetable sowing dates on this site come from a state extension page counted against the city's NOAA frost date, when we have transcribed one.

What is the frost date for cities in zone 7b?

There is no single frost date for zone 7b. Each city page uses the nearest NOAA station with a frost product. The table on this page lists the median last spring and first fall 32°F dates for the cities in this zone on this site. Those dates are 1991 to 2020 medians, not a forecast.

Zone source: USDA Plant Hardiness Zone Map, ZIP tables from the PRISM Climate Group. A city's zone is the ZIP code whose centroid is nearest the city center. Frost dates: NOAA NCEI 1991 to 2020 climate normals.
