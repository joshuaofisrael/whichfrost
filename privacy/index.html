Privacy policy

Last updated 16 Nov 2026. This policy explains how Which Frost (whichfrost.com), a trade name of Joshua Israel Ventures LLC, a Florida limited liability company, handles information.

Analytics

When the public domain is live, pages can load Cloudflare Web Analytics. That service does not use cookies and does not track you across sites. It records basic, aggregated information such as the page, the referrer, the browser, and the country. The preview build does not load it.

Contact

If you email us or use the contact form, your name, email address, and message are used to reply. The form is delivered by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co). We keep messages only as long as needed to respond.

Advertising

The site shows no ads today. If we add ads later, for example through Google AdSense, this policy will be updated first. You would be able to opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings and aboutads.info/choices.

Cookies

Which Frost itself sets no cookies. The site works if your browser blocks them.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log IP addresses for security and operations.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold. Use the contact page. Residents of some states and countries may have additional rights, which we will honor.

Children

The site is a general audience reference and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We will post changes on this page with a new date.
