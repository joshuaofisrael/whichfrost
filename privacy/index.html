Privacy policy

Last updated 19 Nov 2026. This policy explains how Which Frost (whichfrost.com), a trade name of Joshua Israel Ventures LLC, a Florida limited liability company, handles information.

Analytics

Which Frost names the two analytics tools the public site uses.

Cloudflare Web Analytics loads on every public page. It does not use cookies and does not track you across sites. It records basic, aggregated information such as the page, the referrer, the browser, and the country. The preview build does not load it.

Google Analytics 4 loads on a public page only after you choose Allow analytics on the consent banner. The preview build does not load it. The Google tag is not requested until that choice. Until then, Consent Mode v2 defaults keep ad storage, ad user data, ad personalization, and analytics storage denied.

Contact

If you email us or use the contact form, your name, email address, and message are used to reply. The form is delivered by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co). We keep messages only as long as needed to respond.

Advertising

Which Frost loads the Google AdSense script and a google-adsense-account meta tag (publisher ca-pub-5681559774142917) on public pages so the site can be verified for Google AdSense. No ad units are placed on pages today, so you should not see display ads. If we start showing ads, they will be labeled, and this policy and the advertising disclosure will say so. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings and aboutads.info/choices.

Google advertising cookies and your choices

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info/choices. For more information, see how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Cookies

Which Frost does not set its own cookie. The allow or refuse choice is stored in this browser's local storage as wf-ga4-consent, with the value granted or denied, so the banner can stay dismissed. If you allow analytics, Google Analytics 4 may set its own cookies and receive the page address. Consent Mode v2 still defaults ad storage, ad user data, and ad personalization to denied while no ad units are shown.

Choose Cookie settings in the footer to change your choice. Blocking cookies and local storage does not block the frost dates. See the advertising disclosure.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log IP addresses for security and operations.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold. Use the contact page. Residents of some states and countries may have additional rights, which we will honor.

Children

The site is a general audience reference and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We will post changes on this page with a new date.
