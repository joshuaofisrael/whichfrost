Frequently asked questions

How were Florida cities chosen?

Each city is an incorporated place with a 2024 Census population of at least 100,000, whose nearest NOAA normals station with a frost product is within 50 miles of the city center. 27 Florida places are on this site.

Source: NOAA NCEI 1991 to 2020 climate normals. How the station is chosen.
