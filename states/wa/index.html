Frequently asked questions

Which frost date should I use in Washington?

The date columns are the NOAA 50 percent 32°F last spring freeze and first fall freeze at the nearest station. The 50 percent date is the median for 1991 to 2020. It is not a forecast for this year. Open a city for the 10 and 90 percent dates.

How were Washington cities chosen?

Each city is an incorporated place with a 2024 Census population of at least 100,000, whose nearest NOAA normals station with a frost product is within 50 miles of the city center. 10 Washington places are on this site.

Source: NOAA NCEI 1991 to 2020 climate normals. How the station is chosen.
