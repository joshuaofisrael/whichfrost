West Palm Beach, Florida frost dates and planting calendar

By Which Frost. Last verified 16 Nov 2026.

NOAA did not compute a median last spring freeze at 32°F for this station. The median growing season at 32°F is 365 days (the season is at least this long in 50 percent of years).

Station West Palm Beach INTL AP, FL US (USW00012844), 4.5 miles from the city center. Elevation 5.8 m (19 ft, converted from the NOAA meters). NOAA used 28 years for this element. measurement flag Y means there were not enough values to compute it.

The USDA hardiness zone for ZIP 33417, the zoned ZIP whose centroid is nearest the city center (1.7 miles away), is 10b (35 to 40°F).

These figures are 1991 to 2020 climate normals, not a forecast and not a guarantee for this year. A given year can freeze earlier or later. This is not legal advice, not insurance guidance, and not agricultural advice. Census 2024 population estimate: 127,744.

Freeze probability dates

Thresholds are the minimum temperature. 36°F is a light frost in many extension guides, 32°F is a freeze, and 28°F is a harder freeze. Blank results use NOAA's own codes, described under the tables. Spring 90 percent is an earlier date (a freeze is still likely). Spring 10 percent is a later date (only a 10 percent chance the last freeze is still to come).

Frequently asked questions

What does a frost date mean in West Palm Beach?

NOAA did not compute a median last spring freeze at 32°F for this station. When NOAA publishes a date, the spring figure is the chance the last spring freeze happens on or after that day, and the fall figure is the chance the first fall freeze happens on or before that day. A 50 percent date is the median for 1991 to 2020. It is not a forecast for this year.

Why can West Palm Beach have more than one weather station?

This page uses West Palm Beach INTL AP, FL US (USW00012844), the nearest NOAA normals station with a published frost product, 4.5 miles from the city center. Another station in the same area can sit at a different elevation or closer to water, and NOAA can publish a different date there. Which Frost does not average stations together.

What should I do about a late frost in West Palm Beach?

This station does not have a computed 32°F spring date, so there is no median to plant against. Use a forecast for the next few nights, not this normals page, if a freeze is a concern. This is not agricultural advice.
