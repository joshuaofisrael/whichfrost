Columbia, Missouri frost dates and planting calendar

By Which Frost. Last verified 16 Nov 2026.

The median last spring freeze at 32°F is April 5. The median first fall freeze at 32°F is October 31. The median growing season at 32°F is 207 days (the season is at least this long in 50 percent of years).

Station Columbia U OF M, MO US (USC00231801), 0.5 miles from the city center. Elevation 234.7 m (770 ft, converted from the NOAA meters). NOAA used 24 years for this element. Completeness flag R: Representative. NOAA had at least 10 years, and could fill missing months from nearby stations.

Freeze probability dates

Thresholds are the minimum temperature. 36°F is a light frost in many extension guides, 32°F is a freeze, and 28°F is a harder freeze. Blank results use NOAA's own codes, described under the tables. Spring 90 percent is an earlier date (a freeze is still likely). Spring 10 percent is a later date (only a 10 percent chance the last freeze is still to come).

Frequently asked questions

What does a frost date mean in Columbia?

The median last spring freeze at 32°F is April 5. The median first fall freeze at 32°F is October 31. When NOAA publishes a date, the spring figure is the chance the last spring freeze happens on or after that day, and the fall figure is the chance the first fall freeze happens on or before that day. A 50 percent date is the median for 1991 to 2020. It is not a forecast for this year.

Why can Columbia have more than one weather station?

This page uses Columbia U OF M, MO US (USC00231801), the nearest NOAA normals station with a published frost product, 0.5 miles from the city center. Another station in the same area can sit at a different elevation or closer to water, and NOAA can publish a different date there. Which Frost does not average stations together.

What should I do about a late frost in Columbia?

In the 1991 to 2020 normals, NOAA gives a 10 percent chance that the last 32°F freeze in this record is still to come on or after April 21. Planting on the 50 percent date (April 5) still leaves a later freeze possible. This page is not a forecast. If a freeze is predicted after you plant, wait or protect seedlings. That is not agricultural advice.
