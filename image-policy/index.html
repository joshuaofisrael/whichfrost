Image policy

The logo, favicon, and social image are original drawings made for this site: a navy square, a pale snowflake, and a gold center. They are not photographs, not a copy of a weather service mark, and not the USDA zone map.

City pages do not use photos. There is no photograph on this site. If a later page adds an image, it will be original or clearly licensed, this page will name it, and the image will link here.

Which Frost: Which Frost lists median last and first frost dates for U.S. cities from NOAA climate normals, with the USDA hardiness zone and a planting calendar only where a state extension page was transcribed. These are long term averages, not a forecast.
