Image policy

The logo, favicon, and social (og) image are original drawings made for this site: a navy square, a pale snowflake, and a gold center. They are not photographs, not a copy of a weather service mark, and not the USDA zone map.

City, state, and zone lookup pages do not use news photos. Guide articles that carry a hero photo use a single realistic, news-style image generated for that page. Those photos are labeled AI-generated in the caption, and the image links here. They are not photographs of a real garden, person, or place.

If a page needs a photo that is not yet on the site, that page stays out of the live sitemap until the image is added.

Which Frost: Which Frost lists median last and first frost dates for U.S. cities from NOAA climate normals, with the USDA hardiness zone and a planting calendar only where a state extension page was transcribed. These are long term averages, not a forecast.
