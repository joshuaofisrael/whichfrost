About Which Frost

By Which Frost. Last verified 16 Nov 2026.

Which Frost is an independent reference for the last spring freeze and the first fall freeze in large U.S. cities, plus a planting calendar where a state extension has published timing we can cite.

What we publish

Every city page names one NOAA station, the 1991 to 2020 normals, and the distance from the city center. If NOAA did not compute a date, the page says so. We do not fill a gap with a guess.

What we leave out

We do not publish a vegetable date unless it comes from that state's cooperative extension. Many states are still open. See sources and methods.

Something look wrong? Tell us and we will re check the station file.

Which Frost: Which Frost lists median last and first frost dates for U.S. cities from NOAA climate normals, with the USDA hardiness zone and a planting calendar only where a state extension page was transcribed. These are long term averages, not a forecast.
