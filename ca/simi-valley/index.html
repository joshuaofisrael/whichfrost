Simi Valley, California frost dates and planting calendar

By Which Frost. Last verified 16 Nov 2026.

The median last spring freeze at 32°F is January 24. The median first fall freeze at 32°F is December 15. The median growing season at 32°F is 329 days (the season is at least this long in 50 percent of years).

Station Woodland Hills Pierce College, CA US (USC00049785), 11.5 miles from the city center. Elevation 240.8 m (790 ft, converted from the NOAA meters). NOAA used 24 years for this element. Completeness flag R: Representative. NOAA had at least 10 years, and could fill missing months from nearby stations.

The USDA hardiness zone for ZIP 93065, the zoned ZIP whose centroid is nearest the city center (1.4 miles away), is 10a (30 to 35°F).

These figures are 1991 to 2020 climate normals, not a forecast. A given year can freeze earlier or later. This is not legal advice, not insurance guidance, and not agricultural advice. Census 2024 population estimate: 125,778.

Freeze probability dates

Thresholds are the minimum temperature. 36°F is a light frost in many extension guides, 32°F is a freeze, and 28°F is a harder freeze. Blank results use NOAA's own codes, described under the tables. Spring 90 percent is an earlier date (a freeze is still likely). Spring 10 percent is a later date (only a 10 percent chance the last freeze is still to come).

Frequently asked questions

What does a frost date mean in Simi Valley?

The median last spring freeze at 32°F is January 24. The median first fall freeze at 32°F is December 15. When NOAA publishes a date, the spring figure is the chance the last spring freeze happens on or after that day, and the fall figure is the chance the first fall freeze happens on or before that day. A 50 percent date is the median for 1991 to 2020. It is not a forecast for this year.

Why can Simi Valley have more than one weather station?

This page uses Woodland Hills Pierce College, CA US (USC00049785), the nearest NOAA normals station with a published frost product, 11.5 miles from the city center. Another station in the same area can sit at a different elevation or closer to water, and NOAA can publish a different date there. Which Frost does not average stations together.

What should I do about a late frost in Simi Valley?

In the 1991 to 2020 normals, NOAA gives a 10 percent chance that the last 32°F freeze in this record is still to come on or after March 5. Planting on the 50 percent date (January 24) still leaves a later freeze possible. This page is not a forecast. If a freeze is predicted after you plant, wait or protect seedlings. That is not agricultural advice.
